**Common sections:** RDA 0.8 g/kg; 1.6–2.2 g/kg for muscle; per-meal 20–40 g; food lists; calculators.

**What they get wrong / leave out:** Many are supplement sellers and steer to whey. Few show a full Indian day of meals that adds up, few separate "beginner" from "advanced", few explain per-meal spread with Indian meal times, few say what to do if you are overweight (use target weight).

**PAA-style questions:** Is 1 g per kg enough? Is 150 g too much? Can you eat too much protein? Do you need protein on rest days?

**Outline chosen:**
1. Answer: 1.4–2 g/kg (ISSN), ~1.6 g/kg where benefit levels off (Morton 2018); ICMR baseline 0.83.
2. Table by body weight (50–90 kg).
3. If you carry extra weight: use goal weight.
4. Split across 3–4 meals, 20–40 g each, Indian examples (veg and non-veg).
5. Sample day, non-veg/egg version (veg linked to existing guide).
6. Common mistakes; medical caveat.
7. FAQ, sources.
