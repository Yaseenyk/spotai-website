What we saw: "AI जिम ट्रेनर ॲप मराठी" returned only English App Store / Product Hunt listings (Flex AI, F/AI, Gymertia, Coachify). No Marathi page and no app advertising a Marathi interface ranked. People type these topics in English or in Devanagari transliteration, so the title keeps the English terms inside the Marathi sentence: "SpotAI: AI जिम ट्रेनर ॲप आणि जिम मॅनेजमेंट सॉफ्टवेअर".
