**Common sections:** 8–12 tradition vs wide 6–30 range near failure; ~10+ sets/muscle/week; 3–4 sets per exercise; rest 1–3 min.

**What they get wrong / leave out:** Few turn weekly sets into a beginner's actual week; "close to failure" rarely explained in plain terms (reps in reserve); little on starting volume for beginners (lower is enough at first); no translation to a 3-day plan in a busy Indian gym where machines are shared at 7 pm.

**PAA-style questions:** Is 3 sets of 10 enough? How many sets per muscle per week? How long to rest between sets? Should I train to failure?

**Outline chosen:**
1. Answer: 6–15 reps, 2–4 sets per exercise, ~10 hard sets per muscle per week, 1–3 reps from failure.
2. Reps: ranges and what they do; table.
3. Effort: reps in reserve explained.
4. Sets per week by experience; table.
5. Rest between sets.
6. Example: 3-day full body week counting sets.
7. Busy gym tips; progress (link progressive overload).
