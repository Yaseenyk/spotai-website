Checked: 2026-11-02 (web search, standard)

**Phrases:** ಟ್ರೆಡ್‌ಮಿಲ್ ಬಳಸುವುದು ಹೇಗೆ; treadmill walking for beginners; treadmill speed km/h; incline walk

**Why this phrase:** ಟ್ರೆಡ್‌ಮಿಲ್ is the transliteration used in existing kn guides; "ಬಳಸುವುದು ಹೇಗೆ" is the standard Kannada how-to form.
