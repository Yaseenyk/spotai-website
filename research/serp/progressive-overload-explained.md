**Common sections:** definition, ways to progress (weight, reps, sets, tempo, rest), avoid too-fast increases, deloads.

**What they get wrong / leave out:** Many list 8–10 "methods" without telling a beginner which one to use first. Few give a concrete rule (double progression, 2-for-2) with kg increments that match Indian gym dumbbells (2.5 kg jumps) and plates (1.25/2.5 kg). Few explain what to do when stuck, or that you need a log to do it at all.

**PAA-style questions:** How fast should I increase weight? What if I can't increase weight? Is adding reps progressive overload? How often should I deload?

**Outline chosen:**
1. Answer: do a bit more over time; beginners: add reps in a range, then weight (double progression).
2. Why it works (simple).
3. Five ways to progress; which to use first.
4. Double progression worked example (dumbbell press, 2.5 kg jumps).
5. 2-for-2 rule and 2–10% guideline (NSCA/ACSM).
6. When you get stuck: plateau checklist, deload.
7. Log your sets.
8. FAQ, sources.
