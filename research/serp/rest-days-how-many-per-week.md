**Common sections:** 1–3 rest days; active vs passive rest; signs of overtraining; sleep.

**What they get wrong / leave out:** Most do not tie rest days to the split you train (full body vs PPL), give few sample weeks, are cardio/running-heavy, ignore Indian realities (Sunday off, festival weeks, heat, long commutes, late dinners and short sleep). Few explain 48 h per muscle group simply.

**PAA-style questions:** Is it OK to go to the gym every day? Is 2 rest days a week too much? What to do on a rest day? Will I lose muscle if I rest?

**Outline chosen:**
1. Answer: beginners 3–4 rest days (train 3–4), regular lifters 2–3, rarely zero; 48 h per muscle group.
2. Table by experience/days.
3. Sample weeks (full body, upper/lower, PPL).
4. What to do on a rest day (walk, mobility), sleep 7+ h.
5. Signs you need more rest; when to see a doctor.
6. Rest days and streaks; festival and travel weeks.
7. FAQ, sources (WHO 2020, ACSM 2009/2011, sleep consensus).
