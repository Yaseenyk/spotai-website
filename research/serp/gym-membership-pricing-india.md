**What they get wrong / leave out:** The keyword is mostly served by consumer price pages; almost nothing tells an Indian owner how to set prices. No break-even method, no handling of the 5% GST (without ITC) since 22 Sep 2025, no guidance on long-plan discounts vs cash-flow risk, freeze rules, raising prices for existing members, off-peak or student plans. The one "how to price" guide is US-based.

**PAA-style questions:** How much should I charge for a gym membership? Is GST included in gym fees? Should I offer annual plans? How do I raise gym fees without losing members?

4. Step 3: plan ladder with sensible discounts; risks of very cheap annual plans.
