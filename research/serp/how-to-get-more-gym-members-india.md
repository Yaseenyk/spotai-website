**Common sections:** social media, referral programme, free trial, local SEO, partnerships, retention.

**What they get wrong / leave out:** Long, generic lists ("50 ideas") with no priority order; US tactics (direct mail, Facebook-only). Little on what a 100–300 member Indian gym should do first, on following up enquiries within the day, on review rules (no paid/incentivised reviews), on WhatsApp opt-in, or on honest claims (CCPA misleading-ads guidelines). Most are written by software vendors.

**PAA-style questions:** How do gyms attract new members? What is a good gym referral offer? Do free trials work? How do I get my gym on Google Maps?

**Outline chosen:**
1. Answer: Google listing, fast follow-up on enquiries, referrals, and keeping members (retention = growth).
2. Fix the funnel first: enquiry → visit → trial → join; table of what to track.
3. Google Business Profile steps; reviews the right way.
4. Same-day follow-up script; trial that has a plan.
5. Referrals with simple reward (hypothetical ₹ example).
6. Local partnerships: offices, colleges, apartment societies; seasonal timing (Jan, post-Diwali, summer).
7. WhatsApp and Instagram without spam; honest claims.
8. Keep the ones you get (link dropout guide).
9. FAQ, sources.
