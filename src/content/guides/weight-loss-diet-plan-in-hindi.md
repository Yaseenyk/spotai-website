Internet par "weight loss diet plan in Hindi" ke zyadatar result ek fixed chart hote hain: 1,200 calories, meals ki list, aur koi explanation nahi. Woh aapka size, aapki training aur aapke ghar ka khana ignore kar dete hain. Behtar tareeka hai kuch simple rules, jo aapke normal khane ke saath chalein aur zaroorat par badle jaa sakein.

## Basic rules

1. **Chhota sa deficit.** Jitna jalate hain usse thoda kam khayein. ICMR-NIN ki 2024 guidelines lagbhag 0.5 kg prati hafta ko vajan ghatne ki safe raftaar batati hain, aur kehti hain ki diet roz 1,000 kcal se neeche nahi jaani chahiye.
2. **Har meal mein protein.** Isse muscle bachta hai jab aap fat ghata rahe hote hain, aur pet bhara rehta hai.
3. **Weights uthate rahein.** Strength training body ko bolti hai ki muscle rakho. Bina uske, ghatne wale vajan ka zyada hissa muscle se aa sakta hai.
4. **Naapein, phir badlein.** 2 hafte ka weight trend kisi bhi calculator se zyada batata hai.

## Kitna khana hai

Abhi online formulas chhod dein. Yeh karein:

1. Ek hafte tak normal khayein aur jo khaya woh likhein.
2. Usi hafte 3 se 4 subah apna weight lein, toilet ke baad aur khane se pehle. Unka average note karein.
3. Agar weight sthir raha, toh yehi aapka lagbhag maintenance intake hai. Usme se roz 300 se 500 kcal kam karein.

Yeh kami zyadatar teen jagah se aati hai: khana banane ka tel, chai aur mithai ki cheeni, aur chawal ya roti ki doosri helping.

## Har plate ek jaisi banayein

Apni katori aur plate use karein. Lunch aur dinner mein:

- **Aadhi plate: sabziyan.** Sabzi, salad, kosambari, kheere ka raita.
- **Chauthai: protein.** Dal, paneer, chana, rajma, ande, chicken, machhli, dahi.
- **Chauthai: anaaj.** Ek katori pakka chawal, ya 2 medium roti, ya chhoti ho toh ek chawal ek roti.
- **Tel:** har sabzi mein prati vyakti lagbhag ek chammach tel ya ghee, karchhi bhar nahi.

Yeh South Indian, North Indian aur zyadatar ghar ke khane par chalta hai. Aur poora parivaar ek hi khana khata rehta hai.

## Kitna protein

| Guideline | Matra | Kiske liye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICMR-NIN RDA | 0.83 g prati kg body weight roz | Aam adult |
| ISSN position stand | 1.4 se 2.0 g prati kg body weight roz | Jo regularly exercise karte hain |

70 kg ka koi vyakti jo weights uthata hai, lagbhag 100 se 140 g roz ka target rakh sakta hai. Typical Indian diet ke liye yeh kaafi zyada hai, isliye ise baant dein: har main meal mein 25 se 35 g aur ek protein snack. Kidney ki bimari ya koi aur medical condition ho toh protein badhane se pehle doctor se poochein.

## Veg din ka example

| Meal | Kya khayein | Lagbhag protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast | Moong dal chilla (2), pudine ki chutney, 1 katori dahi | 15 se 18 g |
| Lunch | 1 katori chawal, 1 katori dal, 1 katori sabzi, salad | 10 se 12 g |
| Snack | Bhuna chana (30 g), kam cheeni ki chai | 6 se 7 g |
| Dinner | 2 roti, 100 g paneer bhurji ya palak paneer, salad | 20 se 22 g |
| Sone se pehle (optional) | 1 glass doodh ya ek katori dahi | 6 se 7 g |

Isse lagbhag 60 se 65 g ho jaata hai. Training target ke kareeb jaane ke liye dusri katori dal, zyada paneer ya tofu, sabzi mein soya chunks, ya ek scoop whey jodein.

## Non-veg din ka example

Yeh examples hain, prescription nahi. Dishes apne ghar ke hisaab se badlein, bas structure rakhein.

## Training aur rest day

Simple rakhein. Roz lagbhag ek jaisa khayein, protein same. Chahein toh ek portion chawal ya ek kela training se pehle ya baad mein shift kar dein, aur rest day par thoda kam lein.

Gym plan strength par rakhein, hafte mein 3 din, baaki din walking ya cardio. WHO hafte mein 150 se 300 minute moderate activity aur kam se kam 2 din muscle-strengthening ki salaah deta hai.

## Har 2 hafte mein kaise badlein

Abhi ke average weight ki 2 hafte pehle se tulna karein.

| 2 hafte mein badlav | Kya karein |
| --- | --- |
| 0.5 se 1.5 kg kam hua | Aise hi chalte rahein |
| 0.5 kg se kam hua | Roz 200 kcal aur kam karein, zyadatar tel ya chawal se |
| 2 kg se zyada kam hua | Thoda zyada khayein; yeh safe raftaar se tez hai |
| Weight badha | Kuch bhi kam karne se pehle portions aur weekend check karein |

Apne lifts bhi dekhein. Agar strength tezi se gir rahi hai, toh deficit shayad bahut bada hai.

## Kya na karein

- **Bahut kam calorie wale plan** aur sirf fruit ya sirf juice wale din.
- **Dal aur paneer chhodna** kyunki "unme calories hain". Aapko protein chahiye.
- **Fat burner aur detox tea.** Yeh deficit ki jagah nahi lete.
- **Ek baar weight dekh kar ghabrana.** Paani, namak aur periods ek din mein weighing scale ko ek kilo ya zyada hila dete hain.

Agar aapko diabetes, PCOS, thyroid ki problem hai, aap pregnant ya breastfeeding hain, ya roz koi dawa lete hain, toh diet badalne se pehle doctor ya registered dietitian se milein.
