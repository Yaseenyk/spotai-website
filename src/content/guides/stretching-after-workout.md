Most people finish their last set, grab their bag and leave. A short stretch at the end is worth the extra 10 minutes, as long as you know what it can and cannot do.

## What stretching after a workout does

- **Improves flexibility over time.** Stretching most days of the week, for several weeks, increases how far your joints can move.
- **Helps you wind down.** A slow few minutes after training lets your breathing and heart rate settle.
- **Gives you a moment to notice tight spots** before they become a habit, such as tight hips from sitting all day at work.

## What it does not do

- **It does not stop soreness.** A Cochrane review of the research found that stretching before or after exercise made very little difference to muscle soreness over the following days.
- **It is not a cure for pain.** If something hurts, stretching it harder will not fix it. See a doctor or physiotherapist.

## How long to hold each stretch

The American College of Sports Medicine recommends:

| Guideline | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Hold 10–30 seconds | 20–30 seconds is a good target for most stretches |
| About 60 seconds per muscle in total | Repeat a stretch 2–3 times on tight areas |
| Stretch to mild tension | You should feel a pull, not pain |
| At least 2–3 days a week | Daily is better if you want more flexibility |

Breathe slowly through each hold. Do not bounce.

## The 10-minute routine

Do this right after your last set while you are still warm. One round takes about 10 minutes.

| # | Stretch | How | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Easy walk | Walk around the gym or on the treadmill at a slow pace | 1 minute |
| 2 | Standing calf stretch | Hands on a wall, one leg back, heel down, back knee straight | 30 s each leg |
| 3 | Standing quad stretch | Hold one ankle behind you, knees together, hips forward. Hold a rack for balance | 30 s each leg |
| 4 | Hamstring stretch | One heel on a low bench, leg straight, hinge forward from the hips with a flat back | 30 s each leg |
| 5 | Kneeling hip flexor stretch | One knee on a mat, other foot in front, push hips forward gently | 30 s each side |
| 6 | Figure-four glute stretch | Lying or seated, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, pull gently toward you | 30 s each side |
| 7 | Doorway or rack chest stretch | Forearm on a rack upright, elbow at shoulder height, turn your body away | 30 s each side |
| 8 | Cross-body shoulder stretch | Pull one arm across your chest with the other hand | 30 s each arm |
| 9 | Overhead triceps stretch | Hand behind your head, gently push the elbow with the other hand | 30 s each arm |
| 10 | Child's pose | Kneel, sit back on your heels, reach your arms forward on the mat | 30–60 s |

## Change it to fit what you trained

You do not have to do all ten every time. Spend more time on what you worked that day.

| Session | Focus on | Can skip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legs | Calf, quad, hamstring, hip flexor, glute (2 rounds each) | Triceps |
| Chest, shoulders, triceps (push) | Chest, shoulder, triceps, child's pose | Calf |
| Back and biceps (pull) | Child's pose, chest, shoulder; add a gentle hang from a pull-up bar | Calf |
| Cardio | Calf, quad, hamstring, hip flexor | Triceps |

## Stretching in a crowded gym

Many Indian gyms have little free floor space in the evening rush. You can still stretch:

- **Standing stretches only**: calf, quad, chest on a rack upright, shoulder, triceps.
- **Use a bench** for the hamstring stretch, but only one that nobody is waiting for.
- **Do the floor stretches at home**, such as hip flexor, glute and child's pose, before your bath or before bed.

## Common mistakes

- **Bouncing** into a stretch. Move slowly into position and hold.
- **Pushing into pain.** Mild tension only.
- **Holding your breath.** Breathe out as you settle deeper.
- **Rushing.** Five seconds per stretch does very little. Hold for at least 20.
- **Only stretching when sore.** Flexibility improves with regular practice, not one long session.

## Fitting it into your week

If your sessions are already long, stretch on rest days instead. A 10-minute stretch at home counts. See [how many rest days you need](/guides/rest-days-how-many-per-week/) and [how long a gym workout should be](/guides/how-long-should-a-gym-workout-be/).
