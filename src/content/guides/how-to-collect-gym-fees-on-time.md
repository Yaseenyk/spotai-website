Chasing fees is the least favourite job in most gyms. It eats the desk's time, it feels awkward, and the money still comes in late. The fix is rarely a stricter tone. It is a clear system that runs every week.

## Why fees run late

In most Indian gyms, the reasons are ordinary:

- **Fuzzy due dates.** A member joined "around the 10th" and nobody wrote it down.
- **No reminder before the date.** The first message goes out after the fee is already late.
- **Paying is a hassle.** Cash only, or the UPI QR is in the owner's phone and not at the desk.
- **"I'll pay next week."** Said at the desk, accepted, then forgotten by both sides.
- **No one owns the list.** Everyone assumes someone else is following up.

Each of these has a simple fix.

## 1. Write a one-page fee policy

Put it on paper, show it at joining, and have the member sign or accept it. Keep it short:

| Item | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Plans and prices | Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly, all including GST |
| Due date | Renewal is due on the same date as joining |
| Grace period | 5 days after the due date |
| After the grace period | Entry is paused until payment, or a late fee of a fixed amount |
| Payment methods | UPI, card, cash at the desk |
| Freeze and refunds | When a plan can be paused, and refund rules |

A written policy takes the emotion out of follow-ups. You are not asking a favour. You are pointing to what both of you agreed.

## 2. Fix the due date

Pick one of these and stick to it:

- **Anniversary date.** Each member renews on the date they joined. Simple, and dues are spread across the month.
- **Fixed date for everyone.** For example the 1st of each month, with a pro-rata first month. Easier to track, but the desk gets busy at month start.

Longer plans help too. A member on a quarterly or yearly plan has fewer chances to be late. Many gyms price longer plans lower per month to encourage them.

## 3. Make paying take ten seconds

- **UPI QR at the desk**, printed and stuck where members stand, plus in the reminder message.
- **Same name on the QR as your gym's name**, so members do not hesitate.
- **Collect at the desk when they arrive.** The best time to collect is when the member is standing in front of you.
- **Receipts every time**, on WhatsApp or paper, so there is no argument later.
- **UPI AutoPay** lets a member set up a recurring mandate from their UPI app. It suits members who want to forget about renewals. Check how your bank or payment provider supports it.

## 4. A reminder schedule that works

Send reminders on fixed days, every time, for every member.

| When | Message | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 days before | Your plan renews on the 12th. Pay at the desk or by UPI. | Friendly, informative |
| On the due date | Your renewal is due today. Here is the UPI link. | Simple |
| 3 days late | Just a reminder, your renewal is pending. | Polite |
| 7 days late | Your grace period ends soon. After that entry is paused, as per the fee policy. | Firm, clear |
| 15 days late | A phone call from the owner or manager | Personal |

Keep the messages personal: use the member's name, the exact amount and the exact date. A message that looks like a mass forward is easy to ignore.

## 5. The conversation at the desk

When a member says "I'll pay next week", agree a date and write it down in front of them:

> "No problem. Shall I put you down for Saturday? I'll send you a reminder on Friday."

Then actually send it on Friday. Most late fees are not refusals. They are promises nobody followed up.

Never raise dues in front of other members. Ask quietly at the desk or send a private message.

## Grace periods and late fees

Both work if they are written down and applied to everyone. A few points:

- **A short grace period** of 3 to 7 days handles genuine delays like salary dates.
- **Pausing entry** is often more effective than a fine. It is clear, and it does not feel like a penalty for loyal members who are simply busy.
- **Late fees** should be small and fixed. A large fine makes members leave rather than pay.
- **Make exceptions on purpose**, not by accident. A long-time member going through a hard month might get extra time. Note it in the record.

## A 15-minute weekly dues routine

Pick a day, such as Monday morning, and go through this list:

1. Plans renewing in the next 7 days: has the first reminder gone out?
2. Members overdue 1 to 7 days: have they had their reminders?
3. Members overdue more than 7 days: who will call them, and by when?
4. Members overdue who have also stopped coming: they may be leaving, not just late. A call about how training is going works better than a payment reminder.

One person owns this list. If everyone owns it, nobody does.

## GST on gym fees

Gym and fitness centre services attract 5% GST, without input tax credit, from 22 September 2025, following the 56th GST Council meeting. Show prices clearly as inclusive or exclusive of GST, and issue proper invoices if you are registered. Registration thresholds and invoice rules depend on your situation, so check with your CA.
