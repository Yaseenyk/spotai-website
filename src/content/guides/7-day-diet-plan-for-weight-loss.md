Most online "7-day diet plans" are a fixed chart with a calorie number and no explanation, and many promise results that are not realistic. This one is a weekly pattern you can repeat using the food your family already cooks. You can follow it as a **diet chart for women or men**: the structure is the same and the portions change with your size.

## The rules behind the chart

1. **A small deficit.** Eat about 300 to 500 kcal a day less than you burn. ICMR-NIN's 2024 guidelines describe about 0.5 kg a week as a safe pace and say weight-loss diets should not go below 1,000 kcal a day.
2. **Protein in every meal.** It keeps you full and helps you hold on to muscle.
3. **Measured grains and oil.** One cup of cooked rice or two medium rotis per meal, and about one teaspoon of oil or ghee per person per dish.
4. **Half the plate is vegetables.**

## Why not 10 kg in 7 days

Searches like "10 kg weight loss in 7 days diet plan" are common, so it is worth being direct: it is not possible to lose 10 kg of body fat in a week. A scale drop that large is mostly water, and a very low calorie diet costs muscle. The safe pace is about 0.5 kg a week, so a month of steady eating gives you about 2 kg, which stays off far more often than a crash diet.

## The 7-day chart

Portions are examples for a typical adult. Swap any dish for another with a similar protein source.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Snack | Dinner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Moong dal chilla (2), mint chutney, curd | 1 cup rice, dal, sabzi, salad | Roasted chana | 2 rotis, paneer bhurji, salad |
| Tue | Vegetable poha with peanuts, 1 glass milk | 2 rotis, rajma, salad, curd | Fruit and a few almonds | Dal, 1 cup rice, sabzi |
| Wed | 3-egg omelette with vegetables, 1 roti | 1 cup rice, chicken or fish curry, sabzi | Sprouts chaat | 2 rotis, dal, sabzi |
| Thu | Besan chilla (2), curd | 2 rotis, chole, salad | Buttermilk and roasted makhana | Palak paneer, 2 rotis |
| Fri | Idli (3) with sambar, 1 egg or curd | 1 cup rice, dal, sabzi, salad | Fruit | Vegetable soya pulao, raita |
| Sat | Oats upma with vegetables, 1 glass milk | 2 rotis, fish or paneer curry, salad | Roasted chana | Khichdi with vegetables, curd |
| Sun | Paneer or egg paratha (1), curd | Rice, dal, sabzi, a small portion of what the family cooks | Fruit | Light: soup, salad, 2 rotis with dal |

Drink water through the day, and have tea with less sugar instead of cutting tea out.

## How much protein

| Guideline | Amount | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICMR-NIN RDA | 0.83 g per kg body weight per day | General adult population |
| ISSN position stand | 1.4 to 2.0 g per kg body weight per day | People who exercise regularly |

If you walk or train, aim toward the higher figure and spread it across meals, about 20 to 40 g at a time. If you have kidney disease or another medical condition, ask your doctor before raising protein.

## Adjust every two weeks

Weigh yourself 3 or 4 mornings a week and compare the average with two weeks ago.

| Change in 2 weeks | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Lost 0.5 to 1.5 kg | Keep going |
| Lost less than 0.5 kg | Cut about 200 kcal a day, usually oil or rice |
| Lost more than 2 kg | Eat a little more; this is faster than the safe pace |
| Gained | Check portions and weekends before cutting more |

If your gym lifts are dropping quickly, your deficit is probably too big. See the [gym workout plan for beginners](/guides/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners/) for a simple three-day routine, and [calories in Indian food](/guides/calories-in-indian-food/) for the numbers behind common dishes.

## What to avoid

- Very low calorie plans and fruit-only or juice-only days.
- Skipping dal and paneer because they "have calories". You need the protein.
- Fat burners and detox teas, which do not replace a deficit.
- Weighing yourself once and panicking. Water, salt and periods can move the scale by a kilo or more in a day.

If you have diabetes, PCOS, a thyroid condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take regular medicines, see a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
