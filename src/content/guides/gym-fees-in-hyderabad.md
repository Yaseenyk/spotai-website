If you are choosing a gym in Hyderabad, the price on the board is only part of the cost. This guide covers the usual ranges, how plan length changes the price, and what to ask before you pay.

## Typical monthly gym fees in Hyderabad

| Type of gym | Typical monthly fee | What you usually get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget / neighbourhood gym | ₹800 – ₹1,500 | Weights, a few cardio machines, a floor trainer at busy hours |
| Standard gym | ₹1,000 – ₹2,500 | More machines, air conditioning, trainers on the floor |
| Chains and studios with classes | about ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 on long plans | Group classes, app booking, several centres |
| Premium clubs | ₹3,000 – ₹5,000+ | Pool, spa or steam, larger floor, personal training options |

Ranges are drawn from public listings and city guides checked in November 2026. For example, cult.fit's own centre pages in Nizampet and Gachibowli listed effective rates of about ₹956 and ₹1,091 a month on 12-month plans, and about ₹2,048 a month on a 3-month plan, excluding taxes. Prices change often and differ by area, so treat these as a starting point.

## Why longer plans cost less per month

Gyms discount long plans because they get paid upfront and you are more likely to stay. A common pattern:

- **Monthly:** highest price per month, easiest to stop.
- **3 months:** a moderate discount, a good choice for a first membership.
- **6 or 12 months:** the lowest monthly price, but only worth it if you are sure you will keep going.

Many people buy a year in January and stop by March. If you are new to training, start with three months, and renew for longer once the habit sticks.

## Extra charges to ask about

Before you pay, ask the front desk:

1. **Is GST included?** Gym services are taxed at 5 percent since 22 September 2025 (down from 18 percent).
2. **Is there a registration or joining fee?** Some gyms charge one, others waive it during offers.
3. **Locker and towel charges?** Sometimes included, sometimes extra.
4. **Personal training?** Usually priced separately, per month or per session.
5. **Freeze and transfer rules.** Can you pause the plan or move it to a friend?
6. **Refunds.** Most memberships are non-refundable; get the policy in writing.

## How to choose

- **Go at the time you will actually train.** A gym that is quiet at 11 am may be packed at 7 pm.
- **Ask for a trial session or day pass.** Most gyms offer one.
- **Count the squat racks and benches**, not just the treadmills, if you plan to lift.
- **Check how far it is.** A gym ten minutes away beats a better one forty minutes away.
- **Ask how they help new members**: a first plan, form checks, a trainer who checks in.
