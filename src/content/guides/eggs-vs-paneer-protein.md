Eggs and paneer are the two protein foods most Indian gym-goers rely on. The usual web stories give numbers that do not agree, often without saying where they came from. Here are sourced figures, and the comparisons that actually matter for your diet.

## The numbers per 100 g

| Per 100 g | Whole egg | Egg white | Paneer (full-fat, packaged) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protein | 12.6 g | 10.9 g | 20 g |
| Fat | 9.5 g | 0.2 g | 24 g |
| Calories | 143 kcal | 52 kcal | about 310 kcal |

Egg values are for raw egg from USDA FoodData Central. The paneer values are from the label of a common packaged malai paneer. Paneer made at home or from toned milk will differ, so check your pack. Most sources put paneer at 18 to 20 g of protein per 100 g.

So paneer wins on protein per gram. But that is only one way to compare.

## Protein per portion

You do not eat 100 g of egg or paneer by default. Here is what typical portions give you:

| Portion | Protein | Calories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 large whole egg (about 50 g) | about 6.3 g | about 72 kcal |
| 1 large egg white (about 33 g) | about 3.6 g | about 17 kcal |
| 3 whole eggs | about 19 g | about 215 kcal |
| 2 whole eggs + 3 whites | about 23 g | about 195 kcal |
| 50 g paneer (a small katori of cubes) | about 10 g | about 155 kcal |
| 100 g paneer | about 20 g | about 310 kcal |

These are for the raw ingredient. Oil, butter and gravy add calories. A paneer butter masala or an omelette fried in two spoons of oil will be much higher.

## Protein per 100 calories

If you are trying to lose fat, this is the number to watch:

- **Egg white:** about 21 g of protein per 100 kcal
- **Whole egg:** about 8.8 g per 100 kcal
- **Full-fat paneer:** about 6.4 g per 100 kcal

Paneer has a lot of fat, so the calories add up quickly. Eggs, and egg whites in particular, give more protein for the same calories.

## Protein per rupee

Prices change by city and season, so work it out with what you actually pay. Here is the method, using example prices only:

- **Eggs.** Say an egg costs ₹7. One egg gives about 6.3 g of protein, so 10 g of protein costs about ₹11.
- **Paneer.** Say a 200 g pack costs ₹90. That is about 40 g of protein, so 10 g of protein costs about ₹22.

At those example prices, eggs give roughly twice the protein per rupee. Your local numbers will differ, so plug in your own. Prices checked in November 2026 vary widely between shops, apps and cities.

## Which has better-quality protein?

Both are animal proteins and contain all the essential amino acids your body cannot make. Egg protein has long been used as a reference protein in nutrition research, and milk proteins, which make up paneer, also score highly in the FAO's protein-quality methods. For a gym-goer eating enough protein through the day, the difference between the two is small. Total protein matters more.

## So which should you choose?

| If you are... | Better pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trying to lose fat | Eggs, especially whites | More protein per calorie |
| On a tight budget | Eggs, usually | More protein per rupee in most places |
| Vegetarian | Paneer | One of the richest vegetarian sources |
| Trying to gain weight | Either, or both | Paneer adds calories easily |
| Short on cooking time | Boiled eggs | Ready in 10 minutes, easy to carry |

There is no rule that says you must pick one. Many people do eggs at breakfast and paneer at dinner.

## Easy Indian ways to use them

**Eggs**
- Egg bhurji with onion, tomato and green chilli, cooked in one teaspoon of oil
- Two boiled eggs and a banana before or after the gym
- Masala omelette with 2 whole eggs and 2 whites, with one roti
- Egg curry with less oil, served with dal and rice

**Paneer**
- Paneer bhurji, made like egg bhurji
- Grilled or tawa paneer tikka with curd marinade
- Palak paneer or matar paneer with roti
- Paneer in a moong dal chilla or a stuffed paratha with less ghee

## How much protein you need

Most people who train do well on about 1.4 to 2 g of protein per kg of body weight a day, according to the International Society of Sports Nutrition. Our guide on [how much protein you need](/guides/how-much-protein-per-day-for-gym/) shows how to set your own target. If you are vegetarian, the [high-protein vegetarian diet guide](/guides/high-protein-indian-vegetarian-diet-for-gym/) shows how to reach it with paneer, dal, soya and curd.

If you have kidney disease, high cholesterol, diabetes or another condition, check how much protein, fat and how many eggs suit you with your doctor or a registered dietitian.
