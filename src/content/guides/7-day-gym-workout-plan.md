"7-day gym workout plan" can mean three different things, so this guide gives you three schedules and tells you which to pick. All three put rest days where recovery needs them, and all follow the same simple training rules.

## The rules for every schedule

- **Reps and sets:** most sets at 6 to 15 reps, 2 to 4 sets per exercise.
- **Effort:** end each set one to three reps short of failure.
- **Volume:** about 10 hard sets per muscle group per week is a good target. Beginners can start with 6 to 8.
- **Progress:** when you can do the top of the rep range with good form, add a little weight.
- **Rest day:** keep at least one full rest day a week.

(These come from the same research as our guide on sets and reps: Schoenfeld et al. 2016 and 2017, and the ACSM position stand, 2009.)

## Pick your schedule

| You are... | Choose | Training days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New to the gym | 3-day full body | Mon, Wed, Fri |
| 3 to 12 months in | 4-day upper/lower | Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri |
| Experienced, recover well | 6-day push/pull/legs | Mon to Sat |

### 3-day full body (best for beginners and for women starting out)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Squat or leg press, bench or push-ups, row, plank |
| Tue | Rest or a walk |
| Wed | Deadlift variation or hip hinge, overhead press, lat pulldown, lunges |
| Thu | Rest or a walk |
| Fri | Squat or leg press, incline press, row, core |
| Sat | Walk or light cardio |
| Sun | Rest |

This is the routine most new members should use. The [gym workout plan for beginners](/guides/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners/) has more detail.

### 4-day upper/lower

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Upper: bench, row, overhead press, pulldown, arms |
| Tue | Lower: squat, hip hinge, lunges, calves |
| Wed | Rest or walk |
| Thu | Upper: incline press, row, lateral raise, arms |
| Fri | Lower: leg press, hip hinge, split squat, core |
| Sat | Optional cardio |
| Sun | Rest |

### 6-day push/pull/legs

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Push: chest, shoulders, triceps |
| Tue | Pull: back, biceps |
| Wed | Legs |
| Thu | Push |
| Fri | Pull |
| Sat | Legs |
| Sun | Rest |

Each muscle is trained twice a week, which works well, but it needs good sleep and food. If your lifts stall or you feel run down, drop to 4 days.

## Men and women

The training is the same. Women often do well starting with the 3-day full body plan and building up. Choose weights that make the last few reps hard, and increase them gradually. The [gym workout plan for beginners](/guides/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners/) is a good first week for anyone.

## Eating for it

Training works when food backs it up. See the [gym diet plan for muscle gain](/guides/gym-diet-plan-for-muscle-gain/) or the [7-day diet plan for weight loss](/guides/7-day-diet-plan-for-weight-loss/).

If you have an injury, a medical condition, or have been inactive for a long time, check with a doctor before starting.
