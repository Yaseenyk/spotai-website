Protein aapki muscles ko training ke baad repair aur grow hone ka samaan deta hai. Zyadatar gym jaane wale ya toh andaaza lagate hain, ya supplement ke label ka number follow karte hain. Body weight par based ek simple target zyada kaam karta hai.

## Number: prati kg body weight kitne gram

Do figures yaad rakhne layak hain:

- **0.83 g prati kg roz** healthy adults ke liye India ki recommended matra hai (ICMR-NIN, 2020). Yeh basic zaroorat ke liye hai, muscle banane ke liye nahi.
- **1.4 se 2 g prati kg roz** woh range hai jo sports nutrition research regularly train karne wale logon ke liye batati hai (International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2017).

Strength-training studies ke ek bade review mein mila ki extra protein se muscle growth lagbhag **1.6 g prati kg roz** tak badhi, uske baad average mein bahut kam fayda tha (Morton et al., 2018). Isliye zyadatar logon ke liye 1.6 g prati kg ek samajhdaar target hai. 2 g tak jaana theek hai, par zaroori nahi.

| Body weight | Basic (0.83 g/kg) | Gym target (1.6 g/kg) | Upar ki range (2 g/kg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 50 kg | 42 g | 80 g | 100 g |
| 60 kg | 50 g | 96 g | 120 g |
| 70 kg | 58 g | 112 g | 140 g |
| 80 kg | 66 g | 128 g | 160 g |
| 90 kg | 75 g | 144 g | 180 g |

Agar aap abhi shuru kar rahe hain aur roz 40 se 50 g khate hain, toh seedha 120 g par mat jaayein. Do hafte tak roz 15 se 20 g jodein, dekhein kaisa lagta hai, phir aur badhayein.

### Agar vajan zyada hai

Apna current weight nahi, goal weight use karein. Maan lijiye aap 95 kg ke hain aur 75 kg tak pahunchna hai. 95 kg se calculate karne par lagbhag 150 g aata hai, jo khana mushkil hai aur zaroorat se zyada hai. 75 kg se lagbhag 120 g aata hai, jo aasaan hai aur training ko support karta hai.

## Poore din mein baantein

Body protein ko ek bade meal se zyada kai moderate meals mein behtar use karti hai. ISSN har teen se chaar ghante mein lagbhag 20 se 40 g protein suggest karta hai.

100 g ke target ke liye yeh kuch aisa dikhta hai:

- **Breakfast:** 25 g
- **Lunch:** 25 se 30 g
- **Shaam ka snack ya gym ke baad:** 15 se 20 g
- **Dinner:** 25 se 30 g

Typical Indian pattern hai halka breakfast, carb-heavy lunch aur der se dinner. Protein sabse zyada breakfast mein chhootta hai, isliye pehle wahi theek karein.

## Ek sample din: lagbhag 105 se 125 g protein

Yeh 70 kg ke aise vyakti ka example hai jo ande aur chicken khata hai. Veg hon toh dal, paneer, soya chunks aur dahi ko upar ki table se mila kar wahi structure rakhein.

| Meal | Kya khayein | Protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast | Omelette (1 poora anda + 3 safedi) 2 toast ke saath, 1 glass doodh | lagbhag 28 g |
| Lunch | 2 roti, chicken curry (120 g chicken), 1 katori dal, salad | lagbhag 38 g |
| Shaam | Bhuna chana aur moongphali (40 g) ya ek glass doodh | lagbhag 10 g |
| Dinner | Chawal, machhli curry (120 g machhli), 1 katori dahi | lagbhag 30 g |
| Sone se pehle (optional) | 100 g paneer bhurji ya 1 glass doodh | lagbhag 8 se 18 g |

## Galtiyan jo protein kam rakhti hain

1. **Roti aur chawal ko protein maan lena.** Unse kuch protein milta hai, par 3 roti aur sabzi ki plate se sirf 10 se 12 g milta hai.
2. **Dal ki chhoti katori.** Ek chhoti katori 6 se 7 g deti hai. Do poori katori se fark padta hai.
3. **Sirf dinner mein bada protein.** Raat ko 60 g aur subah lagbhag kuch nahi, use karna bhi mushkil aur nibhana bhi mushkil.
4. **Shake se sab theek karna.** Protein powder ek convenience hai. Khane se target pura ho jaaye toh zaroorat nahi.
5. **Andaaza lagana.** Zyadatar log apna protein zyada samajhte hain. Teen aam dino ka khana likhein aur jod lein. Kaagaz par ya SpotAI ke food log mein, jisme Indian foods hain aur roz ka protein jodta hai.
