Most neighbourhood gyms in India already grow by word of mouth. A referral program simply makes that word of mouth more frequent, and rewards the members who bring people in. Done well, it brings in members who already know someone at the gym, which helps them stay.

## What makes a referral program work

- **One clear offer.** "Bring a friend: you both get 2 weeks free." If it needs explaining, it will not be shared.
- **Both sides get something.** Members are happier to share an offer that helps their friend.
- **Reward after the friend stays.** Not on sign-up day.
- **Asked at the right time.** Right after a member hits a milestone, not during a complaint about the AC.
- **Always on.** A program that runs all year is remembered. A one-week offer is forgotten.

## Reward ideas that fit Indian gyms

| Reward | For | Why it works | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 weeks to 1 month added to the plan | Member | Costs you little; keeps them coming | Cap how many months one member can stack |
| Joining or admission fee waived | Friend | Removes the first barrier to signing up | Do not waive it so often it means nothing |
| Free first week | Friend | Lets them try before paying | Collect details and a start date |
| 2 free personal training sessions | Either | Shows off your trainers; may lead to paid PT | Trainers must have the time |
| Gym t-shirt, shaker or towel | Member | Visible; advertises the gym | Order sizes in advance |
| Discount on next renewal | Member | Encourages renewal too | Keep the percentage modest |
| Bigger reward at 3 referrals | Member | Rewards your best ambassadors | Keep the tiers simple |

Avoid cash rewards in most cases. They attract people who refer for money, not because they like the gym, and they cut straight into your margin.

A note on tax: GST on gym services is 5% (without input tax credit) since 22 September 2025. Under the GST law, a discount that is shown on the invoice at the time of sale reduces the value GST is charged on. Ask your CA how to record free days or vouchers.

## When to give the reward

Set a simple rule, write it on the poster, and stick to it:

> The member gets their reward when their friend has paid for at least one month and checked in 8 times.

This protects you from friends who join and never come, and it nudges your member to bring the friend along, which helps the friend build the habit. Check-in records make this easy to verify.

## When to ask members

Good moments to mention the program:

- **After a member's 25th or 50th visit**, or a new personal best.
- **When they renew.** They have just decided the gym is worth paying for.
- **January**, when friends talk about getting fit.
- **After Diwali and wedding season**, when many people want to restart.
- **Before summer holidays**, for college students going home or coming back.

Train the front desk to say it the same way every time: "If any of your friends wants to join, bring them along. You both get two weeks free once they have done 8 visits."

## How to run it on paper or WhatsApp

You do not need special software.

1. **Poster at the front desk** and near the water cooler, with the one-line offer.
2. **Ask every new member "Who told you about us?"** and write it down.
3. **Keep one sheet** with these columns:

| Date | New member | Referred by | Plan paid | Visits in first month | Reward given |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

4. **Check it every Monday.** Give rewards on time and message the member when you do.
5. **Send a short WhatsApp message** to existing members once a month at most, only to those who have agreed to hear from you. See our guide to [WhatsApp marketing for gyms](/guides/gym-whatsapp-marketing/).

Do not ask members to hand over a list of friends' phone numbers for you to call. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you need a person's own consent to use their personal data. Let members share the offer themselves, and let the friend contact you.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Rewarding on sign-up.** You will pay for members who never come back.
- **Hidden conditions.** If the member feels cheated, you lose two people.
- **Forgetting to pay out.** A late reward does more harm than no program.
- **Only rewarding the referrer.** Friends respond better when they get something too.
- **Running it, then going silent.** Mention it at renewals and milestones every month.

## Make sure referred members stay

A referral is only worth it if the new member sticks around. Pair them with their friend's training time, give them a first-week plan, and notice if they stop coming.
