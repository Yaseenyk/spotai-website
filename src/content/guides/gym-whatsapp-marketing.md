Almost every member and every enquiry in India is on WhatsApp. That is why gyms use it, and also why it goes wrong. Ten offers a month, forwarded posters and late-night broadcasts get your number muted or blocked. A few useful, personal messages do the opposite: they bring people in and keep them renewing.

## The rules first

Before you send anything promotional, get consent.

- **WhatsApp's policy.** WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy says a business may contact people only if they have given their number and agreed to receive messages from that business. Repeated complaints or blocks can get your number restricted.
- **India's data protection law.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires consent that is free, specific, informed and clear, and lets people withdraw it as easily as they gave it. Its rules are being phased in, so check the current position with your CA or lawyer.
- **Honour opt-outs.** If someone says "stop", remove them from broadcasts the same day.

### How to collect opt-in

Add one line to your enquiry form and joining form:

> "I agree to receive updates, offers and reminders from [Gym name] on WhatsApp. I can stop them any time by replying STOP."

Keep a tick box or signature. For walk-in enquiries, ask out loud and note it.

## Business app or API?

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business Platform (API) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Free | Paid per message, through a provider |
| Best for | Single gyms, up to a few hundred members | Larger gyms, chains |
| Broadcasts | Lists of up to 256, delivered only to people who saved your number | Larger sends using pre-approved templates |
| Automation | Greeting, away message, quick replies, labels | Automated flows, integrations |
| Setup | Minutes | Days, needs business verification |

Most neighbourhood gyms should start with the free app and move only when it clearly limits them.

## Set up the Business app properly

- **Profile:** gym name, address with map pin, timings, phone and a clear logo.
- **Catalogue:** your plans with prices, inclusive of GST.
- **Labels:** New enquiry, Trial booked, Member, Renewal due, Stopped coming.
- **Quick replies:** fees, timings, location, trial booking. Type "/" to send them.
- **Greeting message** for first-time chats, with timings and a question: "Would you like to book a free trial session?"
- **Ask every member to save the gym's number** at joining, so broadcasts reach them.

## What to send: a monthly message calendar

| When | Message type | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Monthly update: timings, holidays, new batch or class | All opted-in members |
| Week 2 | Useful tip: a short exercise demo, a protein idea with Indian food | All opted-in members |
| Week 3 | A challenge or event: monthly visits challenge, a Sunday group session | All opted-in members |
| Any time | Renewal reminders, 5 days before and on the due date | Individual members |
| Any time | "We have not seen you this week" | Members not checked in for 7 days |
| Within 1 hour | Reply to every enquiry | Leads |
| Day 2 and day 7 | Follow-up after a trial or enquiry | Leads who did not join |

That is three broadcasts a month. Everything else is one-to-one.

## Message examples

**Enquiry reply (within an hour)**

> "Hi Sneha, thanks for asking about [Gym name]. Monthly is ₹1,500 and quarterly ₹4,000, including GST. We are open 5:30 am to 10 pm. Would you like a free trial session this week? Mornings after 10 are usually quiet."

**Missed week (personal, from a trainer)**

> "Hi Arjun, Ravi here from the gym. Haven't seen you this week, all okay? If evenings are hard right now, the 7 am batch is quiet. Want me to keep a spot on the squat rack for you on Thursday?"

**Monthly update (broadcast)**

> "[Gym name] update for March: open 6 am to 1 pm on Holi. New women-only batch from 11 am, Monday to Saturday. This month's challenge: 12 visits in March gets your name on the board. Reply STOP to stop updates."

The names, prices and timings here are examples only. Use your own. Fee reminder wording is in our [gym fee reminder templates](/guides/gym-fee-reminder-message/).

## What not to send

- Forwarded posters and quotes with no connection to your gym
- More than one broadcast a week
- Messages before 8 am or after 9 pm, unless replying to a question
- Members' photos, weights or results without their written permission
- Anything that shames someone about their weight or attendance
- Dues reminders in a group, ever

## WhatsApp groups: use them carefully

Groups feel friendly but expose everyone's phone number to everyone else, and they turn noisy fast. If you run one, keep it small and specific, such as a running club or a challenge group, make admins-only posting the default, and let people leave without fuss. Do not add members without asking.

## Measure what matters

Do not judge WhatsApp by how many people you sent to. Track:

- **Replies** to broadcasts and personal messages
- **Trial bookings** from enquiries, and how many joined
- **Members back within 7 days** after a "missed you" message
- **Renewals on time** after reminders
- **Blocks and opt-outs**, which tell you when you are sending too much

Review this once a month. Drop message types that get no replies. Do more of what brings people through the door.
