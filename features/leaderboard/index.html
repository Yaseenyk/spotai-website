For members

Lose steam after week two?

See the kilos add up and let your gym see you turn up.

Three boards this week: visits, kilograms, streak. Lifetime totals and badges on your gains screen. Buddy suggestions with the reason they match.

How it works

  1. 1

    Gains

    Lifetime kilograms, reps and workouts, this week’s numbers, personal bests.

  2. 2

    Badges

    Kilograms from 1,000 to 100,000; reps from 500 to 25,000; workouts from 10 to 250.

  3. 3

    Leaderboard

    Join with your first name. Visits, kilograms and streak, reset each week. You versus the buddy you train with most.

  4. 4

    Buddies

    Suggestions with the reason: same start, same hours, a shared goal. Requests, chat and groups.

What you get

  • Opt in, opt out

    Your numbers are private until you join the board. Leave and they go private again.

  • Separate consent for Connect

    Buddy matching and chat are their own opt-ins, adults only. Trainers moderate and can endorse helpers.

  • Helper badge

    Members trainers endorse show up first in buddy suggestions.

Today’s limits

  • Leaderboard and Connect are for members aged 18 and over.
  • Boards are per gym; there is no cross-gym ranking.

Questions

How does the gym leaderboard work?

Opt in, first names only. Three boards: visits this week, kilograms this week, current streak. Leave any time.

Who can message me?

Only buddies you accepted. Chat is a separate consent and trainers moderate it.
