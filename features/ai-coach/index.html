SpotAI/AI coach

For members

No one spotting your squat?

Prop your phone on the rack. The coach counts reps and calls your form.

Pose tracking runs on the phone with MediaPipe. Each rep is checked for three things, and at the end of the set you get one tip written from templates your gym’s trainer approved.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set the phone down

    Side-on or at 45°, leaning on the rack or a bench, whole body in frame.

  2. 2

    Do the set

    The counter runs on body landmarks. Reps count as you lift; no tapping.

  3. 3

    Read one tip

    Knee valgus, shallow depth or forward lean, flagged per rep. One cue for the set, in English, Hindi or Bengali.

  4. 4

    Tell it if it was wrong

    Tap “This was wrong” or “Helpful”. Trainers see each template’s wrong-rate and retire the bad ones.

What you get

  • Three checks per rep

    Knee valgus (knees caving in), shallow depth, and excessive forward lean, each scored per rep from joint angles.

  • Trainer-approved cues

    Every tip is a template a trainer reviewed in the staff console. The model gateway only words it; if the gateway is down, the exact template text is used.

  • Nothing leaves but counts

    The server receives a rep count and per-rep flags. Never a frame, never a landmark stream.

  • Clips are a separate switch

    Saving a clip of your set is its own consent, off by default. Delete any clip, any time, from the app.

Today’s limits

  • Squats only today. Deadlift and bench press are next; every exercise already has a how-to and a short.
  • Camera features are for members aged 18 and over.
  • Works best with the whole body in frame and steady light.

Questions

Does the AI coach send my video to the cloud?

No. Pose tracking runs on your phone. Only rep counts and form flags leave the device, never frames.

Which exercises does the form check cover?

Squats today, with knee valgus, shallow depth and forward lean detected per rep. More exercises are coming.

Who writes the tips?

Your gym’s trainer approves the templates. The app picks one that matches the flag on your set.
