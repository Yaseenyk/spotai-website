For members

Walk in with no plan?

Check in and the app tells you what to train today, then logs every set.

The suggestion follows the twice-a-week-per-muscle guideline: the muscle group that is most rested comes first. You can always pick something else.

Get early access Questions

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a session

    Strength by muscle group, cardio, cycling or mobility. The app marks the one it suggests and says why.

  2. 2

    Tick the exercises

    Each one shows how many times you did it this week and when you last did it, so the gaps are obvious.

  3. 3

    Log each set

    Reps and kilograms for strength; timed sets for cardio and mobility. Watch a 30-second short if you forgot the move.

  4. 4

    Done for today

    The home screen shows the session ticked off, your streak and visits this week.

What you get

  • Suggested, not forced

    The rule looks at your last seven days and picks the muscle group trained least, then the one rested longest. Override it with one tap.

  • Exercise library with shorts

    Every exercise has a how-to and a short from a well-known coach, opened inside the app.

  • Your plan, if you have one

    Follow a programme day by day, or train freestyle. Rest days are marked.

  • Record a set with the camera

    On squats, hand the set to the AI coach and it counts for you.

Today’s limits

  • Sample plans are reviewed by a trainer before launch; today’s plan is a demo.
  • Shorts play inside the app and need a connection.

Questions

Can I ignore the suggestion?

Yes. Pick any session or any exercises. The suggestion is a starting point.

Does it work offline?

Logging works offline and syncs when you are back online. Shorts need a connection.
