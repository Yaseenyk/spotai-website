Who came, who owes, when it is busy. One screen, and members who pay in the app.

How it works

  1. 1

    Today at a glance

    Arrivals as they happen with dues on the name, active members, memberships ending this week, collected this month against last.

  2. 2

    Dues

    One list, days overdue, a reminder button that respects each member’s consent. UPI payments from the member app land here with a reference.

  3. 3

    Insights

    Who visited in the last 30 days, who came back, visits per week, who uses the AI coach, busy hours by weekday. Figures from fewer than five members are hidden.

  4. 4

    Members and plans

    Plans, statuses, check-in history, complaints with their reference numbers.

What you get

  • Front desk console

    Check-in by QR or by name. Staff see who is in and who owes, nothing else.

  • Trainer console

    Review the AI coach’s tip templates with each flag’s wrong-rate, endorse helpers, moderate chat and groups.

  • Complaints with a reference

    Members file from the app and get a number. You answer from the dashboard. Required under India’s DPDP rules.

  • Face check-in, your call

    An optional entrance device with a liveness check and encrypted templates, nothing in the cloud.

  • One database, no leaks

    Every table is scoped to your gym with row-level security. Another gym can never read your members.

  • Week-long offline demo

    Trial the member app with no server: the whole API answers from the phone.

Today’s limits

  • Dues reminders go to a WhatsApp stub in the demo; nothing is sent.
  • Pilot gyms are onboarded by us today; self-serve sign-up comes later.

Questions
